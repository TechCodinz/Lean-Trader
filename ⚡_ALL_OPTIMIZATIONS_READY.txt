════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEBSOCKET_DATA_FEED:
- Connects to exchange WebSocket
- Gets price updates every 100ms (not 1-60 seconds!)
- Catches dips and pumps instantly
- Result: Perfect entry and exit timing

SMART_ORDER_MANAGER:
- Places LIMIT orders (0.01% fee) instead of MARKET (0.15% fee)
- Waits 30s for fill, then uses market if needed
- Auto-adds stop-loss and take-profit

- Result: 1 trade instead of 5 = 75% fee reduction
