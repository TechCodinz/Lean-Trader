## 🧠 HOW IT WORKS

### Engine Registration
Every engine joins the swarm and shares its state:

```python
# At startup, each engine registers:
self.collective_swarm.register_engine('MICRO_WALLET_GROWER')
self.collective_swarm.register_engine('ULTRA_RARE_ENGINES')
self.collective_swarm.register_engine('BRAIN_SYSTEM')
self.collective_swarm.register_engine('HIVEMIND')
self.collective_swarm.register_engine('OPPORTUNITY_SCANNER')
```

### Swarm Voting (5 Perspectives)
Every signal gets voted on by 5 different perspectives:

1. **Technical Vote** - Price action, confluence
2. **Fundamental Vote** - News, trending, sentiment
3. **Risk Vote** - Recent performance, win rate
4. **Timing Vote** - Session optimal, market hours
5. **Momentum Vote** - Multiple engines confirming

**Requires 3/5 votes to approve trade!**

### Example Voting:
```

Result: 5/5 votes YES → Swarm Confidence: 80%
```
